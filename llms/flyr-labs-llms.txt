# FLYR Hospitality

> FLYR Hospitality is an AI-first hotel revenue management system (RMS) and commercial strategy platform. It replaces spreadsheets, rules-based revenue management software, and fragmented business intelligence stacks with a single AI-native platform for hotel revenue and commercial teams. Trusted by 2,300+ properties worldwide, rated 4.6/5 on HotelTechReport.

FLYR Hospitality offers three core products — Optimize (AI-driven dynamic pricing and rate optimization), Insights (hotel business intelligence and reporting), and Planning (hotel revenue forecasting and budgeting) — alongside Groups, a dedicated tool for hotel group pricing and displacement analysis. The platform is designed for hotel groups and chains, independent hotels, upscale and luxury properties, limited-service and economy hotels, hostels and hybrid stay properties, resorts, and short-term/long-term stay operators.

## Platform

- [Optimize](https://www.flyrhospitality.com/optimize): AI hotel revenue management software with always-on dynamic pricing — hourly rate optimization, intelligent demand evaluation, and automated price decisions with full oversight and alerting. Replaces manual RMS workflows for revenue managers.
- [Insights](https://www.flyrhospitality.com/insights): Hotel business intelligence platform — natural language querying, automated reporting, unified data platform with near real-time refresh, and market benchmarking. Eliminates siloed reporting and gives every team a single source of truth.
- [Planning](https://www.flyrhospitality.com/planning): Hotel revenue forecasting and budgeting platform — continuously updated demand forecasts, scenario stress-testing, ensemble forecasting with uncertainty quantification, and budget vs. actual tracking.
- [Groups](https://www.flyrhospitality.com/groups): AI-powered hotel group pricing software — displacement cost analysis, margin-first visibility, what-if scenario modelling, and alternative date recommendations to help sales teams close deals faster at higher margin.
